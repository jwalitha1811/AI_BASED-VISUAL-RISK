Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. We are committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you use Digital Twin AI:

  • Account data – username, email, password hash (securely stored).
  • Financial data – income, expenses, savings, transactions you choose to log.
  • Study data – study hours, subjects, productivity scores.
  • Fitness data – activity types, duration, calories burned.
  • Goals – descriptions and target dates.
  • Chat messages – questions and responses with the AI assistant.

How We Use Your Data

  • To build your personal dashboard and show insights about your finances, studies and fitness.
  • To give you AI-based suggestions and predictions.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account when needed.

Data Security

Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to keep your data safe from unauthorized access, but no system is completely risk free.

Your Rights

  • You can view the data we hold about you.
  • You can correct or update your information.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data.
