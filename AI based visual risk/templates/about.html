About Us

Learn more about Digital Twin AI and why we built it.

What is Digital Twin AI?

Digital Twin AI is a web app that creates a digital version of your daily life. You add your money, study and fitness details, and the app uses AI to show patterns, point out possible risks and give simple suggestions to help you plan better.

Our Goal

Most people track their spending, studies and health in different places, if they track them at all. We wanted to bring everything together in one place and make it easy to understand, so you can spot problems early and work towards your goals.

What You Can Do

Track Finances

Log income, expenses and savings and see where your money goes.

Monitor Studies

Record study hours and subjects and check your productivity.

Follow Fitness

Keep a record of your activities, time spent and calories burned.

Set Goals

Write down your goals with target dates and keep an eye on them.

Risk Insights

See visual summaries that highlight areas that need attention.

AI Assistant

Ask questions in chat and get answers based on your own data.

How It Works

  • Create a free account and log in.
  • Add your financial, study and fitness details.
  • View your dashboard and risk insights.
  • Chat with the AI assistant for suggestions.
